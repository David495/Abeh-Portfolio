MY APPROACH

Impact should create possibility.

I am passionate about initiatives that help people see new possibilities for themselves and their communities.

For me, humanitarian work is about more than offering support in a moment. It is also about helping create the conditions for people to learn, develop skills, find their voice, and take meaningful steps towards their goals.

Through initiatives such as A Chance to Dream and skills-acquisition training for girls, I am committed to contributing to a future where opportunity is not limited by circumstance.

I believe lasting progress happens when we listen to communities, work together, and focus on practical ways to make a difference.
