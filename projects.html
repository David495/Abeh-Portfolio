HOPE & POSSIBILITY

Every future begins with the chance to imagine more.

A Chance to Dream is an initiative connected to the belief that people should be able to imagine a better future and have support as they work towards it.

Its spirit is about opening up possibilities, encouraging ambition, and helping people see that their circumstances do not have to define the limits of what they can become.

✳

The vision
Make room for hope, aspiration, and practical opportunity.

02

SKILLS & EMPOWERMENT

Practical skills can open doors to new possibilities.

This skills-acquisition initiative aims to train 300 girls, helping them access practical learning and build confidence in their abilities.

By investing in skills, the initiative recognises the importance of giving girls tools they can continue to develop and use as they pursue their ambitions.

↗

The goal
Support 300 girls through skills development and empowerment.
